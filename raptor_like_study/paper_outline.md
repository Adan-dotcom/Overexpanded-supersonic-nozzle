# Proposed paper

## Working title

CEA-informed machine learning prediction of shock-induced boundary-layer
separation in representative LOX/CH4 rocket nozzles.

## Claims we may be able to support

- A reproducible public-parameter LOX/CH4 nozzle dataset.
- A comparison between equilibrium and frozen combustion-product models.
- A geometry-aware neural predictor for separation onset.
- Uncertainty estimates and an out-of-geometry test set.

## Claims we must not make without evidence

- Exact prediction of a current proprietary Raptor nozzle.
- Universal separation prediction from one turbulence model.
- Accuracy beyond the validation data and physics models.

## Required validation

- Mesh refinement.
- RANS versus URANS sensitivity.
- Equilibrium versus frozen chemistry sensitivity.
- Comparison with published experimental overexpanded-nozzle data.
- A held-out geometry family, not only random CFD rows.
